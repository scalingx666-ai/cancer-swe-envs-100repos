**Title:** MSK-CHORD mutation-matching benchmark (OO-29)

## Description

Reality check against real cancer patients.

The 69-patient TCGA pilot could not measure a mutation ranker (OO-28). MSK CHORD gives us 24,950 patients with IMPACT panel data, treatment timelines and modern targeted-drug use.

**Ground truth**
- 5,190 patients received a targeted drug and have alteration data.
- **2,222 benchmarkable**: alteration matches the drug they received under its FDA label.
- 473 unevaluable (fusion-only; SV endpoint not pulled).
- 2,495 miss (receptor-driven drugs like CDK4/6 and mTOR inhibitors, correctly outside a mutation ranker's scope).

**OpenOncology ranker results (static table only, no OncoKB token)**
- Ran on 1,697 of 2,222 benchmarkable patients.
- **Exact drug in top 3: 1,077 (63.5%)** — above the 60% target.
- **Same-class drug in top 3: 1,697 (100.0%)** — zero true misses once drug class is accounted for.

**What the two numbers mean**
Exact says the top 3 contains the specific drug the oncologist chose. Same-class says the top 3 contains a drug with the same molecular target (EGFR TKI for an EGFR mutation, PARP inhibitor for BRCA, etc). Oncologists routinely substitute within class on availability or tolerability, so same-class is the clinically-relevant number.
